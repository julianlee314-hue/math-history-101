Concept 102

Counting Outcomes

IV · Renaissance
三3 · ALGORITHM四4 · PRINT
三

3 · ALGORITHM

IndiaBaghdad/Islamic-PersianChina

Counting outcomes enumerates possibilities—dice casts, lineup permutations, combinatorial choices. Indian combinatorics and later Islamic and European chance problems built systematic counting. Outcome trees branch the futures. Before probability numbers, honest listing already disciplined talk of chance. Systematic listing was already a civic discipline before odds wore decimal clothes.

四

4 · PRINT

Atlantic Scientific RevolutionItalian Commercial/Renaissance

Printed problems on cards, dice, and lots spread combinatorial counting into genteel and commercial cultures. Factorials and combinations gained notation. Counting outcomes became the counting house of uncertainty. Shared print exercises let distant classrooms list the same possibility trees. Combinatorial counting left specialist texts and entered parlor and classroom print.
