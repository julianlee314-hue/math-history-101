Concept 105

Spread / Variation

VI · Industrial
四4 · PRINT六6 · COMPUTE
四

4 · PRINT

British Industrial/EngineeringFrench Enlightenment/Revolutionary

Spread describes how widely data scatter—range, deviation, variance. Nineteenth-century statistics and error theory in print insisted center without spread misleads. Variation is often the signal (diversity) or the enemy (noise), depending on the civic goal. Whisker plots teach the story of scatter beside the average. Center without spread misleads—print-era error theory insisted on saying how wide.

六

6 · COMPUTE

American/Global Computational

Statistical software computes dispersions instantly; six-sigma culture industrialised variance talk. Visualizations of spread guard against averages that hide inequality. Computation made spread metrics cheap; interpreting them still costs attention. Fast variance numbers still need a human who asks what the scatter means. Spread metrics left specialist error theory and entered everyday analytics.
