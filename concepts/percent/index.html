四

4 · PRINT

Italian Commercial/RenaissanceBritish Industrial/Engineering

Percent—“per hundred”—is commercial ratio wearing a hundred-based costume. Italian merchant practice and later printed interest tables made % ubiquitous. Tax, discount, and risk still speak percent as a public dialect. Percent wheels convert between fractions, decimals, and hundredths for everyday trade. Percent wheels and printed tables made hundredths a public dialect of risk and discount.

6 · COMPUTE

American/Global Computational

Spreadsheets and dashboards compute percents instantly, sometimes hiding bad denominators. Relative change literacy remains the human filter. The print-era commercial shortcut became the default KPI format of modern organizations. Fast percent buttons do not replace asking “percent of what?” Percent left the interest table and became the default language of KPIs.
