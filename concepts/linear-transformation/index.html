5 · MECHANIZE

Central Europe 19th-c Abstract

Linear transformations map vectors so combinations survive—rotations, projections, shears. Matrix representation makes them calculable. Klein-style thinking tied geometries to groups of linear and related maps. Transform lenses show geometric verbs instruments and formulas could share. Transform lenses in teaching echo the idea: change the view, track what survives.

6 · COMPUTE

American/Global Computational

Every neural layer and graphics shader leans on linear transforms plus nonlinear garnish. Eigenviews reveal a transform’s true axes. Computation performs linear maps billions of times per frame or training step. Rotate, project, shear—once geometric verbs—now run as matrix multiplies at frame rate.
