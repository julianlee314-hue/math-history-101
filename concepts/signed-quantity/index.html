三

3 · ALGORITHM

IndiaBaghdad/Islamic-PersianChina

Signed quantities extend number with direction—debt and credit, left and right, below zero. Indian and Chinese mathematicians used negatives with varying philosophical comfort; Islamic algebra sometimes spoke of them cautiously. Thermometers later made signed temperature visceral. Opposite direction became numeric. Named steps let apprentices repeat the claim’s method without reinventing each motion.
