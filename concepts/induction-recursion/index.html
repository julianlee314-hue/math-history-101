Concept 116

Induction & Recursion

IV · Renaissance
四4 · PRINT六6 · COMPUTE
四

4 · PRINT

Atlantic Scientific RevolutionCentral Europe 19th-c Abstract

Mathematical induction proves infinite families by a base step and a hereditary step—formalized clearly in early modern Europe though foreshadowed earlier. Recursion defines by self-reference. Print made induction a standard template of number theory and algebra. Shared templates let distant classrooms prove “for all n” the same way. Base step plus hereditary step became a standard printed proof template.

六

6 · COMPUTE

American/Global Computational

Recursive procedures and inductive proofs about programs grew together in computer science. Proof assistants check inductive arguments mechanically. The print-era template became the verification style of software math. Base case plus hereditary step now audits code as well as integers. Induction left number-theory templates and entered software verification.
