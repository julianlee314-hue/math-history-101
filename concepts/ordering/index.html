Concept 004

Ordering

I · Origins

Ordering is noticing that numbers sit in a line: after three comes four, and five is farther still. People ordered long before they drew number lines—clay tokens lined up, notches read left to right, ranks of soldiers and rows of jars. What the number line adds (as a teaching tool mostly in the last few centuries) is a picture of order and distance at once. Place a chip at 4 and another at 9, and “between,” “before,” and “farther” become visible. In Mathera, the number line turns the abstract sequence of counting into a landscape you can walk.
