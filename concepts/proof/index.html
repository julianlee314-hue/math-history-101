二

2 · WRITE

Greek/Hellenistic

Proof is demonstrative certainty from agreed starts—Euclid’s template for two millennia. Greek proof culture taught that a diagram plus reasons can compel assent across cities. Mathematics became more than clever tricks. Written argument let strangers test necessity without standing in the original workshop. Elements scrolls symbolized public necessity: assent compelled by reasons, not by status.

七

7 · INTELLIGENCE

American/Global ComputationalCentral Europe 19th-c Abstract

Formal proof systems and assistants (Lean, Coq, Isabelle) check arguments at scales humans mistype. Hilbert’s dream meets silicon scrutiny. Proof remains a social and logical act; intelligence tools are new scribes that refuse to skip lines. Humans still choose which theorems matter; machines help refuse gaps. Proof assistants refuse skipped lines; humans still choose the theorems.
