5 · MECHANIZE

Central Europe 19th-c AbstractAtlantic Scientific Revolution

Continuity means no jumps—intermediate values attained, small input changes yielding small output changes in the ε–δ sense. Nineteenth-century analysis separated continuity from differentiability with famous monsters. Smooth-looking chalk curves needed new definitions. Continuity ribbons picture unbroken motion that instruments and proofs had to take seriously. Continuity ribbons pictured unbroken motion that ε–δ definitions had to capture.

6 · COMPUTE

American/Global Computational

Topology and computational geometry track continuity in spaces far from the number line. Discrete data only approximates continuous ideals. Machines sample; continuity remains the mathematical hope about what happens between samples. Pixels and floats approximate; the continuous claim still guides the model. Continuity guides the model even when machines only hold samples.
