Infinite Process & Convergence

二

2 · WRITE

Greek/Hellenistic

Infinite processes haunted Greek thought—Zeno’s paradoxes, exhaustion methods that approach without claiming a final infinite sum lightly. Limit funnels picture narrowing. Writing forced clarity about what “going on forever” could responsibly mean in proof. Strangers could argue the approach without standing in the original workshop. Exhaustion methods approached without lightly claiming a finished infinite sum.

5 · MECHANIZE

Central Europe 19th-c AbstractAtlantic Scientific Revolution

Cauchy, Weierstrass, and peers rigorized convergence so infinite processes became trustworthy tools. Analysis textbooks still drill ε-arguments born in that repair shop. Infinite methods gained passports only after convergence tests stamped them. What once felt mystical became a checklist of limits you could teach and check. Convergence left philosophical unease and entered ε-checklists in analysis.
