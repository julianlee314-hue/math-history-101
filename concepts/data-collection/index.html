一

1 · MARK

MesopotamiaEgypt/Eastern MediterraneanChina

Data collection is organized noticing—census counts, skywatches, harvest tallies, tribute lists. Early states lived on data marks pressed into clay or inked on papyrus. Collecting is already a political act: who is counted, who is missed, which unit is used. Without collection, later statistics have nothing to chew.
