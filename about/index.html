About

Math History 101 is a public curiosity cabinet of mathematical ideas told as civilization-style tech history. Concepts are short lessons; artefacts evolve like shadowed Pokémon lineages—you can count the level-ups, but the full era timeline stays intentionally foggy.

Colors mark historical eras (Origins through Modern), not Mathera learner eras. Dates are honest ranges, never fake invention years.

Batch 1 covers Counting through Distance. Concepts 13–144 appear as muted placeholders so the mess of 144 is visible from day one. Built as static files for GitHub Pages.

Companion to the Mathera curriculum prototype.
