About

Math History 101 is the public companion site for the Mathera History Network—a curiosity cabinet of mathematical ideas linked to the Mathera app by stable Concept IDs.

Epochs (Chinese 一–七: MARK, WRITE, ALGORITHM, PRINT, MECHANIZE, COMPUTE, INTELLIGENCE) describe how humanity stores, spreads, and automates math. They are distinct from Mathera learner Eras (Roman I–VII Count…Space).

The Concept Wall is a 12×12 trophy shelf of all 144 concepts. Pixel art (8×8 → 16×16 → 32×32 mastery) is designed elsewhere; tiles here are paper/ink placeholders ready for art. Batch 1 (Counting through Distance) has full history pages; 13–144 show real names as “coming.”

Twelve Worlds sketch the civilizational stages—from Mesopotamian clay tablets to American/global computation.

Editorial law: follow what survived—roots + breakthrough + transmission + industry + modern form. Not first-person-wins. Dates use honest ranges.

Companion to the Mathera curriculum prototype. Static GitHub Pages; relative URLs.
